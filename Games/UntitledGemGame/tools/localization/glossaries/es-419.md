# Glossary: es-419 (Latin American Spanish, neutral)

Style: player addressed as tú ("Haz clic", "tu flota"); buttons as infinitives ("Aplicar",
"Comprar +1 celda de energía"). Names use sentence case ("Pozo gravitacional"), except proper
words (Génesis, Midas, Tesla) and the five ship types, which are always capitalized.
LatAm vocabulary: mouse, agregar, costo, capacitor, riel, sismo, picada, liviano, qué tan.
Numbers and decimals kept as in English (0.5, 10,000), as in Mexico.

## Signal targets
"Increases {0}." / "Reduces {0}." are rendered "Aumento de {0}." / "Reducción de {0}.", and
"Total: {0}{1}% {2}" as "Total: {0}{1}% de {2}", so every lowercase target phrase is a noun
phrase without a leading article ("velocidad de todos los recolectores").
