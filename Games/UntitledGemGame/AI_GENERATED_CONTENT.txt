AI-generated content
====================

Assets in this game that were made by an AI model rather than taken from an
asset pack or drawn by a person. Add an entry when generated content is
committed; remove it if a person redraws the asset.

Effects drawn by code at runtime (shape glows, beams, trails) are not listed.

Each entry: path (relative to this folder), what it is, how it was made, date
added, and whether the game uses it.

Made by Claude (Anthropic) in Claude Code
-----------------------------------------

Content/Localization/*.json (every language except en.json)
  Translations of all player-facing text into 18 languages, written by Claude
  from the English keys (one translator per language, each with its own
  glossary). Not reviewed by native speakers yet. The English tooltips in
  Content/Data/upgrades*.json were also shortened by Claude the same day.
  Added 2026-10-07. Used in game.

RawAssets/Steam/parts/
  High-resolution cut-outs of game objects, as source material for composing
  store art by hand in Krita (overview.jpg shows a sample of each): the home
  base cruiser from seven camera angles, bare and with its six turrets mounted
  at the game's hardpoints (also as separate layers on the same canvas); the
  five weapon turrets; the six harvesters; crystal ore pickups in the eight gem
  colours; the core shard, prestige orb and crystal planet; rockets, harpoon
  and engine flame. These are the in-game sprites listed in this file (and the
  Synty ship and turret models) rendered again at 8 to 20 times the game's
  resolution by the same tools/blender/prerender.py pipeline, so the same
  split applies: Synty POLYGON meshes and palette, with AI-written materials,
  lighting and glow compositing, and fully AI-written crystals, prestige orb
  and engine flame. Rendered by Claude headless in Blender 5.3 with
  tools/blender/capsule_parts.py and packed by tools/build_capsule_parts.py.
  Added 2026-11-20. Not used by the game. Store art made from these parts
  contains AI-generated content; move its entry out of the "not AI-generated"
  list below when that happens.

RawAssets/Steam/parts/capsule_1232x706/
  The same parts resampled to the sizes they have in the 1232x706 main
  capsule (home base about 850 px long; sizes in its README.txt), by
  tools/build_capsule_set.py, plus two things made for it:
  ship/<view>/engine_flames.png, the engine exhaust as a layer on each ship
  view's canvas, laid out by Claude's script from the game's nozzle positions
  and the fusion-drive flame strips listed at the top of this file; and
  effects/laser, cannon, railgun and harpoon, 50 stills of the weapon effects
  the game draws with shaders at runtime (mining laser, Overheat Surge and
  Planet Cracker beams, incendiary cannon rounds, railgun charge, muzzle
  flash, round and ion trail, Arc Harpoon lance, rings and lightning). Those
  are NumPy ports of LaserBeam.fx, IncendiaryRound.fx, RailgunBolt.fx,
  ArcAnchor.fx and ModuleEffects.fx written by Claude in
  tools/build_capsule_fx.py, with a few edges softened so a still has no
  seams; effects/cannon/muzzle_flash.png has no shader behind it and is
  Claude's own. Added 2026-11-20. Not used by the game.

RawAssets/Steam/main_capsule_scene.png
  Main capsule (1232x706) as a scene: the home base firing its mining laser
  and cannons into the crystal planet, ore spilling out and harvesters
  flying in. Composed by Claude in Krita through the Krita MCP server,
  following the layout of main_capsule.kra. The space background, logo text
  and its glow, frame and background rocks (hidden) are cloned from
  main_capsule.kra (the user's own Krita work); the ship, planet, ore,
  harvesters, beams, tracers and flames are the parts in
  parts/capsule_1232x706/ listed above. Claude's own layers, drawn with
  NumPy/Pillow from inside Krita: the planet's damage (molten laser scars,
  and fracture cracks ported from Screens/GameScreen.PlanetCracks.cs,
  checked against capture-mode stills of a core fracture), planet shadow,
  atmosphere glow, rim light, highlight and impact scorch; sparks, hit
  ripples and streaks; hull lights, ship rim glow and drop shadows; ore
  glow, trails and glints; star glints, vignette, logo backdrop and the
  baked bloom. Added 2026-11-20. Not used by the game; store art.

Made by Claude (Anthropic) in Antigravity
-----------------------------------------

Content/Textures/Gems/plasma_orb.png
  World gem pickup sheet (128x128 px, 2x2 grid of four 64x64 px variants):
  faceted crystal-ore clusters bursting out of dark planetary crust rock with a
  soft outer glow aura, packed into GemShader.fx's surface channels so the
  crystals and glow take each gem type's tint while the rock base stays dark.
  Uses rock/asteroid meshes from the Synty POLYGON Generic and POLYGON Sci-Fi
  Space packs combined with procedural crystal shards, materials and lighting
  written in tools/blender/prerender.py (render_plasma_orb, CRYSTAL_ORE_VARIANTS),
  then packed by tools/build_gem_surface.py from RawAssets/Prerender/plasma_orb.
  Added 2026-10-15, replaced 2026-10-16. Used in game.

Content/Textures/Prerender/Planets/planet_crystal.png
  The mined planet, one revolution in 77 frames of 288 px (9-column sheet).
  The model (SM_Env_Planet_01) and the land mask texture are from the Synty
  POLYGON Sci-Fi Space pack; the material approximating Synty's planet shader,
  its colours and the lighting were written by Claude in
  tools/blender/prerender.py (render_planet_spin, CRYSTAL_PLANET). Packed by
  tools/build_prerender_sheet.py from RawAssets/Prerender/planet_crystal.
  Added 2026-10-15. Used in game.

Content/Textures/Gems/core_shard.png
  Core Shard icon and world pickup sprite (256x256 px): a low-poly faceted
  fragment of the planet's molten inner core with a scorched planetary rock
  crust, glowing magma-gold polygon facet seams, incandescent solar-gold and
  white-hot molten craters/pockets, and a warm golden corona. Uses the
  SM_Env_Astroid_Holes_01 mesh from the Synty POLYGON Sci-Fi Space pack, with
  Cycles/EEVEE multi-pass rendering (beauty, normal, Voronoi pocket and
  per-polygon ID/crater passes) and magma-core compositing written in
  tools/blender/prerender.py (render_core_shard; raw passes in
  RawAssets/Prerender/core_shard).
  Added 2026-10-16, replaced 2026-10-18. Used in game.

Content/Textures/Gems/prestige_orb.png
  Prestige Point icon and flight sprite (64x64 px): a Singularity / Time-Loop
  Orb with a deep black-violet event horizon, white-lilac photon ring,
  gravitationally lensed upper and lower arches, and a tilted swirling
  violet/lilac accretion disk modulated by a 3D faceted accretion ring rendered
  in Blender. Written in tools/blender/prerender.py (render_prestige_orb; raw
  pass in RawAssets/Prerender/prestige_orb).
  Added 2026-10-16. Used in game.

Content/Textures/Prerender/Weapons/rocket_main.png
Content/Textures/Prerender/Weapons/rocket_mini.png
