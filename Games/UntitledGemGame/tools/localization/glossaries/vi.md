# Glossary: Vietnamese (vi)

Style: upgrade, ship, weapon, ability, module and signal names in Title Case (each word
capitalized, as in VN game localizations); descriptions and menu items in sentence case.
Numbers keep the game's invariant format (0.5, 10x); "s" kept for seconds in compact HUD text,
"giây" in sentences. Plural keys: one form (no "|").
