# Beyond the Belt: fresh shorts slate

12 October 2026. Ten concepts based on the current gameplay code and data.
Status: proposed; no new footage recorded. Numbering restarts at 01.

The old 39 short folders (05–43), legacy TrailerAssets shorts, exports, review
images, old ZIP archive and stale capture catalog have been removed locally.
Existing trailers and Steam-page assets remain separate projects.

## Direction

Show the incremental loop: mine the planet, collect gems, buy upgrades, grow the
fleet, extract the core, and return stronger. Each short should make one change
and its payoff easy to see. Plain captions, real gameplay and game sound, fullscreen
9:16, roughly 15–25 seconds. Use the existing licensed soundtrack when cutting.

Stage comparisons honestly: label different progression states when needed;
never imply elapsed play time. Any counters must come from capture logs. Keep
harvesters fueled with AutoRefuel. Show today's HUD and panels when they explain
the change. Each short can finish with the game name; verify the live Steam page
before adding a wishlist CTA. Frame important action above the bottom 20% and
outside the right 12% of the portrait frame.

## 01 — One click starts the loop (20–24 s)

**Hook:** A cannon shot hits the planet and knocks gems loose immediately.

**Shots:** 0–5 s: tight view of the ship and planet, click to fire, then collect
what falls. 5–9 s: buy Plasma Repeater in the current upgrade tree. 9–17 s:
return to the same framing and let automatic fire and the first harvester work.
17–24 s: match-cut to a later fleet with a brief “Later in the game” label.

**Payoff / caption:** “Mine. Collect. Upgrade.” The same loop grows in scale.

**Capture:** Start after the crash intro; the intro is not the hook. Beginning
already owns the first Drifter. Use the real purchase, not a fake unlock.

## 02 — The next weapon changes the planet (18–22 s)

**Hook:** A charged Railgun shot strikes the planet.

**Shots:** Cut back to a basic cannon for 3–4 s, then introduce Mining Laser,
Arc Harpoon, rockets and Railgun in short isolated takes. Keep the planet in
the same place and show each weapon firing before moving on.

**Payoff / caption:** Weapon names only; end on a wider shot of them working together.

**Capture:** Stage successive weapon unlocks; disable the other weapons in
isolated shots. Keep full rocket paths in frame and reset lingering effects
between takes. Do not call the entire sequence a single run or a timed journey.

## 03 — A fleet that keeps bringing gems home (18–22 s)

**Hook:** A loaded harvester delivers gems to the ship.

**Shots:** Follow one Drifter leaving home, collecting near the planet and
returning. Match-cut to several ship classes covering the field, then pull
back to reveal a larger fleet. Keep deliveries and the real gem counter visible.

**Payoff / caption:** “One harvester.” → “A growing fleet.”

**Capture:** Show current planet navigation and ship behavior. Prioritize a
clear delivery cycle over a crowded shot where individual ships disappear.

## 04 — The core cracks open (15–20 s)

**Hook:** Open just before the planet swallows the loose gem field.

**Shots:** Let the contraction, fracture and eruption play continuously. Follow
the Core Shard out of the crack, collect it, then hold briefly on the damaged
planet and the replenished field.

**Payoff / caption:** “Crack the core. Collect the shard.”

**Capture:** Stage a shell-free planet near a fracture, or use the capture
fracture action. Frame the planet, eruption and shard together. This is a
staged showcase, not a claim that one particular shot always causes a fracture.

## 05 — Scan the faults, hit the weak points (15–18 s)

**Hook:** Fault Scan marks the planet's surface.

**Shots:** Briefly show the current command button, activate Fault Scan, and
hit marked spots with clearly readable fire. Let each burst release gems.
Finish with the collectors bringing the resulting haul home.

**Payoff / caption:** “Fault Scan.” → “Hit the marked points.”

**Capture:** Use the current command name and effects. Keep the near side
of the planet large enough for the marks and bursts to read on a phone.

## 06 — One hit starts a chain reaction (15–20 s)

**Hook:** A detonation ripples through molten spots on the planet.

**Shots:** Show cannon hits leaving molten craters, then a rocket or Railgun
hit setting one off. Let Chain Reaction propagate through nearby spots;
finish on the gems and collection that follow.

**Payoff / caption:** “Molten craters.” → “Magma Detonation.” → “Chain Reaction.”

**Capture:** Use Thermite Rounds, Magma Detonation and Chain Reaction in a
legal talent setup. Exclude Ionized Magma, which conflicts with that setup.
Isolate the initiating hit so the cause and effect remain visible.

## 07 — Grow a ship system (20–24 s)

**Hook:** An upgraded Genesis Pulse fills the field with rings and gold.

**Shots:** Cut to a basic Genesis Pulse in the same framing. Show a short
current Ship Systems panel shot, spending Power Cells on the relevant nodes.
Return to an upgraded pulse, then let Graviton Cascade pull the haul home.

**Payoff / caption:** “Genesis Pulse.” → “Upgraded with Power Cells.”

**Capture:** Respect system row requirements and the Auxiliary Power unlock.
Compare legal early and advanced setups. The final combination should have
room to spawn gems and time for the collection effect to finish.

## 08 — Find a module, see it work (18–22 s)

**Hook:** A sealed module opens to reveal its actual name and rarity.

**Shots:** A readable reveal; equip the module in the current Shipyard; close
the panel and follow a ship using it. Finish on its visible effect or a clear
before/after delivery, depending on the module.

**Payoff / caption:** “Discover. Equip. Collect.”

**Capture:** Choose the module after previewing its actual effect. Use a
module with a legible gameplay payoff, and show the correct ship class and
slots. Do not guarantee a legendary result or an invented drop rate.

## 09 — A signal makes the next haul better (18–22 s)

**Hook:** A scan reveals three real signal choices.

**Shots:** Show the offered bonuses at phone-readable size, select one that
improves gem earnings, and return to a controlled collection shot. If possible,
show another stack and its real accumulated bonus in the signal panel.

**Payoff / caption:** “Scan. Choose a bonus. Keep it growing.”

**Capture:** Pick a currently available signal from the fresh catalog; use the
exact text and actual value. For a comparison, hold fleet, modules and other
upgrades constant. Label staged comparison takes and avoid invented earnings.

## 10 — Extract the core, return stronger (22–26 s)

**Hook:** The core collapses and pulls the ship into the next time loop.

**Shots:** Cut back briefly to holding Extract Core in the real HUD. Play the
collapse; show earning/spending a prestige point in the current talent tree;
return to mining with that talent's visible effect. End with a later tier's
expanded space, labeled as a later loop.

**Payoff / caption:** “Extract the core.” → “Keep the talent. Start again.”

**Capture:** Use an extractable run. Show prestige talents and free tier rewards
accurately: Command Center, Ship Systems, Shipyard and signals unlock at later
tiers, rather than all on the first extraction. Avoid compressing those unlocks
into a supposedly immediate first reset.

## Suggested first three

Start with **01** to explain the current game, **04** for its strongest planet
payoff, and **06** for a readable upgrade combination. Together they cover the
basic loop, spectacle and depth without repeating the same montage.

Before recording, regenerate the catalog from the current build, prepare scenes
for the selected concept, and review low-resolution previews for framing,
current unlock rules and clear payoffs. Any missing capture action belongs in
Capture/, with no changes to normal gameplay or the player's save/settings.

## 15 — Heavy ordnance showcase (18 s)

**Hook:** Shattering the planet's outer crust in one clean fracture.

**Shots:** 0–3.33 s: shattering the outer shell. 3.33–6.67 s: 3D rockets firing

## 16 — Meet the five fleet classes (18 s)

**Hook:** Five distinct 3D harvester ship classes shown in isolated takes.

**Shots:** 3 s each of Drifters roaming the field, Rimrunners sweeping the outer
ring, Seekers hunting high-tier gems, and Prospectors pulling in clusters with
tractor beams, followed by 6 s of Trove Hunters warping home with full loads.
