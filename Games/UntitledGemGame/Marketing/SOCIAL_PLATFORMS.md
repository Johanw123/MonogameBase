# Beyond the Belt: platform hashtags and recurring posting opportunities

Researched **14 October 2026**. Suggested hashtag sets and posting frequency
are editorial starting points for Team Jape, not verified trending tags or
promises of reach. Platform rules and community traditions are identified
separately. See [the main posting guide](README.md) for caption templates and
export preferences, and [the Reddit list](REDDIT.md) for community-specific rules.

## Useful hashtag bank

| Purpose | Relevant options | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player discovery | `#IndieGame`, `#IndieGames`, `#IncrementalGame`, `#IncrementalGames`, `#SpaceGame` | Actual gameplay and the mining/fleet/progression loop. Pick one broad and one specific tag; test singular/plural variants rather than using all of them together. |
| Idle audience | `#IdleGame` | When the clip actually demonstrates automation. Do not imply offline progression or fully hands-off play unless those claims are true. |
| Development | `#GameDev`, `#IndieDev`, `#GameDevelopment` | Design decisions, development progress and making-of content. More likely to address peers than the intended player audience. |
| Visual/engine | `#PixelArt`, `#MonoGame` | Pixel-art-focused visuals or MonoGame technical discussion. Do not use Unity/Godot engine tags for this project. |
| Identity | `#BeyondTheBelt`, `#TeamJape` | Grouping our own content. These are brand identifiers; do not assume an existing discovery audience. |
| Weekly conventions | `#TrailerTuesday`, `#WishlistWednesday`, `#ScreenshotSaturday` | Tuesday trailers, Wednesday wishlist posts and Saturday visual updates respectively. |

Use the game's name and genre in ordinary text as well. Hashtags are optional
labels, not a substitute for showing the gameplay or explaining the hook.

## X / Twitter

**Audience and angle:** Good place to test short visual hooks and join indie
game conversations. Show manual mining becoming a fleet, World Eater collecting
gems, or the planet cracking. Use a native clip/GIF or screenshot plus a short
explanation and one destination link. This format recommendation is our judgment.

**Hashtags:** Start with zero to two. Player-facing example:
`#IndieGame #IncrementalGame`; development example: `#GameDev #MonoGame`.
On an event day, replace one ordinary tag with the event tag. X recommends
no more than two hashtags as a best practice, not a hard limit.
[Official hashtag guidance](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/how-to-use-hashtags)

**Recurring community opportunities:** These are open community conventions,
not official X events or guaranteed curator features.

| Day | Tag | Beyond the Belt post angle | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday | `#TrailerTuesday` | A gameplay trailer explaining mining, upgrades and fleet growth. | Use an actual trailer or coherent gameplay teaser. Follow any host thread's requirements if replying to a showcase. |
| Wednesday | `#WishlistWednesday` | A satisfying fleet clip plus the live Steam page and a wishlist ask. | Only when the page is public and wishlisting works. Pair with `#IncrementalGame` or `#IndieGame`. |
| Saturday | `#ScreenshotSaturday` | Core fracture, a busy fleet, or a module reveal with a short description. | Screenshots, GIFs and gameplay videos all suit this convention. Pair with `#IndieGame`. |

Screenshot Saturday and Wishlist Wednesday are documented in this
[indie-game marketing guide](https://gamedevelopermarketing.com/how-to-promote-your-indie-game/).
Trailer Tuesday has direct developer usage, for example this
[developer's trailer post](https://itch.io/blog/964021/ark-axiom-trailer).
Check recent native search results before selecting showcase accounts to reply
to; this research does not certify any repost account's current activity.

**Practical rules:** Hashtags cannot contain spaces or punctuation. Public
account posts can appear in hashtag search. Use a direct game/store URL when
that is the goal, and avoid repeated copy-paste promotional replies.

Example for Saturday:

```text
One mining ship becomes a fleet in Beyond the Belt, our incremental space game.

[Attach fleet clip]

#ScreenshotSaturday #IndieGame
```

Add the real store URL and a wishlist ask only when the page is live. Remove
the attachment placeholder when composing the post.

## Instagram Reels

**Audience and angle:** Use the finished vertical shorts to show one satisfying
moment, with a clear first-frame hook. Explain the genre in the caption and
give a single next step. Our recommended starting set is three to five relevant
tags, staying within the announced five-tag limit for posts/Reels.
[Limit announcement coverage](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)

**Hashtag sets:** Gameplay:
`#IndieGame #IncrementalGame #SpaceGame #BeyondTheBelt`.
Development: `#GameDev #IndieDev #MonoGame #BeyondTheBelt`.
Saturday: `#ScreenshotSaturday #IndieGame #IncrementalGame #BeyondTheBelt`.
Add `#PixelArt` only when the art is the focus; it can take the fifth slot.

**Event days:** Try Screenshot Saturday for a visual update. Trailer Tuesday
and Wishlist Wednesday can supply a caption theme, but the evidence reviewed
here does not establish an Instagram-run indie promotion event or equivalent
reach to X. Treat them as experiments, not required posting days.

**Practical rules:** Use a public account for discovery. Ordinary caption URLs
are generally not clickable; point to a working profile link or use a Story
link where available. Only use a caption-link feature if the account actually
has it. See [the main guide](README.md#instagram) for the existing link notes.

## TikTok

**Audience and angle:** Put the payoff early: one ship becomes many, or World
Eater sweeps up a gem cloud. Include the game name and a phrase such as
"incremental space mining game" in the caption/on-screen text so the premise
is understandable without decoding tags.

**Hashtags:** Start with three to five relevant tags as our working set:
`#IndieGame #IncrementalGame #SpaceGame #BeyondTheBelt`.
For an automation clip, swap `#SpaceGame` for `#IdleGame`; for a development
explanation, use `#GameDev #IndieDev #IncrementalGame`.
Do not assume `#FYP` or `#Viral` supplies a targeted gaming audience.

**Event days:** No platform-run weekly indie showcase was established in this
research. Test `#ScreenshotSaturday` or `#TrailerTuesday` when the content fits,
but prioritize relevant topics and the clip's hook over matching X's calendar.
Check TikTok's [Creative Center Trends guidance](https://ads.us.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-trends?lang=en&redirected=2)
and [Creator Search Insights](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/creator-search-insights?lang=en)
where available for current topic demand; these suggestions are not a live
trend ranking.

**Practical rules:** Say "link in bio" only if the account has a working
website link. TikTok documents a 1,000-follower threshold for personal-account
website links; account type and regional availability can matter.
[Account/link guidance](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/growing-your-audience/switching-to-a-creator-or-business-account)
Otherwise use a follow ask. Check the composer for the account's current tag limit.

## YouTube Shorts

**Audience and angle:** Use a title explaining the feature, for example
"From One Mining Ship to a Fleet | Beyond the Belt". Put the game/genre and
where to find it in the description. The existing shorts are ready-made footage
for this channel; clear titles matter more to our plan than calendar labels.

**Hashtags:** Our suggested set is two or three in the description:
`#IndieGame #IncrementalGame #BeyondTheBelt`.
For a Saturday visual update, replace one with `#ScreenshotSaturday` if useful.
`#Shorts` is optional; it is not a discovery guarantee.

**Event days:** No YouTube-run weekly indie trailer/wishlist event was
established. Publishing a Saturday showcase or Tuesday trailer is an editorial
schedule; it does not enroll the video in an official platform event.

**Practical rules:** Hashtags must be relevant, cannot contain spaces, and
excessive/misleading tagging can lead to removal from search or removal of the
video. More than 60 hashtags causes YouTube to ignore all hashtags on that
content. URLs in Shorts descriptions/comments are **not clickable**; point
to the channel profile link, or a related YouTube video when eligible.
[Hashtag rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en) ·
[Clickable-link rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en)

## Facebook Page / Reels / groups

**Audience and angle:** On the Team Jape Page, add a little context for people
seeing the game for the first time. Reels can reuse the vertical gameplay edits;
a regular Page post can describe a feature and link to its destination.

**Hashtags:** Our suggested starting set is zero to three:
`#IndieGame #IncrementalGame #BeyondTheBelt`.
Facebook's Reel composer supports hashtags, which help discovery in search.
[Facebook Reel instructions](https://www.facebook.com/help/www/2862139500770200)

**Event days:** Screenshot Saturday can be a theme for Page posts. In groups,
look for their specific self-promotion day or weekly showcase thread; rules
vary by group. No platform-wide indie event was established here, and a
`#TrailerTuesday` tag does not grant permission to advertise in a group.

**Practical rules:** Use a public audience for discovery. Verify link behavior
in the Reel preview and use the Page website link if needed. Follow each
group's rules about links, AI disclosures, promotional frequency and designated
threads before posting. This guide does not certify individual Facebook groups.

## A manageable starting rhythm

This is our proposed schedule, not a platform requirement. Two strong posts
per week are a reasonable starting workload; add a trailer slot when there is
a worthwhile trailer or announcement.

| Day | Core idea | Adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday, when useful | Trailer or coherent teaser | X `#TrailerTuesday`; r/playmygame Trailer Tuesday using its own format; vertical edit for Reels/TikTok/Shorts. |
| Wednesday, once Steam is live | Explain the game and invite wishlists | X `#WishlistWednesday`; elsewhere use the same clear player-facing premise and each platform's working link route. |
| Saturday | One visually strong gameplay feature | X `#ScreenshotSaturday`; adapt the feature clip for Instagram, TikTok, Shorts and Facebook. |

Rotate fleet growth, mining weapons, core fracture and modules rather than
reposting the same clip every week. Follow subreddit cooldowns independently
of this calendar. Log the post URL, clip, tags, views and useful actions
(profile visits, store visits or wishlists where measurable). Compare results
within each platform and keep the combinations that bring interested players.

The Reddit AI rules are in [REDDIT.md](REDDIT.md). This platform guide is about
organic formats, hashtags and events; it is not a review of every platform's
AI-labeling or advertising policy. No posts have been published.
