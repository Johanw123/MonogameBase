# Glossary: Japanese (ja)

Style: upgrade/talent/module descriptions in plain form or noun-ending (体言止め), e.g.
「ドリフターの飛行速度が上昇。」. System/error messages and confirmations in polite form
(です/ます). Buttons as short nouns/verbs (戻る, 設定, 適用). Full-width punctuation
（、。：！？（）…）, half-width digits and %, no spaces between words. "Nx" multipliers in prose
become 「N倍」; short popups keep "x{0}". Seconds suffix "s" becomes 「秒」.
