# Beyond the Belt: fresh shorts slate

25 October 2026. Eight focused shorts based on the current gameplay code, 3D ships,
planet rendering, and `GuiSkin` HUD.

## Direction

Show the incremental loop: mine the planet, collect gems, buy upgrades, grow the
fleet, extract the core, and return stronger. Each short makes one change and its
payoff easy to see. Plain white captions, real gameplay, Synthwave music mixed
with game sound effects, fullscreen 9:16, 15–21 seconds.

Stage comparisons honestly: label different progression states when needed;
never imply elapsed play time. Keep harvesters fueled with `AutoRefuel` and full
fuel capacity so behavior showcases never stall waiting for fuel. Show the current
`GuiSkin` HUD and panels when they explain the change. Frame important action
above the bottom 20% and outside the right 12% of the portrait frame.

## 01 — First click: Mine, upgrade, collect (21 s)

**Hook:** A cannon shot hits the planet and knocks red gems loose immediately.

**Shots:** 0–3 s: tight view of the ship and planet, click to fire, then collect
what falls. 3–7.5 s: buy Plasma Repeater (`AC1`) and Harvester Count (`HC1`) in
the current `GuiSkin` upgrade tree. 7.5–12 s: return to gameplay as automatic
fire and the first harvesters work. 12–21 s: match-cut to a developing fleet with
a “Later in the game” label and end card.

## 02 — Heavy ordnance showcase (18 s)

**Hook:** Shattering the planet's dark outer crust in one clean fracture.

**Shots:** 0–3.33 s: shattering the outer shell. 3.33–6.67 s: 3D rockets firing
and blooming into Cluster Warheads. 6.67–10 s: Arc Harpoon cyan tethers and
Railgun shockwave ring. 10–18 s: full late-game weapon barrage, labeled “Later in the game”.

## 03 — Meet the five fleet classes (18 s)

**Hook:** Five distinct 3D harvester ship classes shown in isolated takes.

**Shots:** 3 s each of Drifters roaming the field, Rimrunners sweeping the outer
ring, Seekers hunting high-tier gems, and Prospectors pulling in clusters with
tractor beams, followed by 6 s of Trove Hunters warping home with full loads.

## 04 — Core fracture & Core Shard (16 s)

**Hook:** Open just before the planet swallows the loose gem field.

**Shots:** 0–8 s: let the contraction, fracture and eruption play continuously,
then click the Core Shard as it emerges from the crack. 8–16 s: hold on the
damaged planet and the replenished field being collected.

## 05 — Magma chain reaction (17.5 s)

**Hook:** A detonation ripples through molten spots on the planet.

**Shots:** 0–2.5 s: chain reaction hook on the planet surface. 2.5–7.5 s: cannon
hits leaving glowing molten craters. 7.5–12 s: a rocket/Railgun hit setting off
Magma Detonation and Chain Reaction across nearby spots. 12–17.5 s: gems and
fleet collection across the field.

## 06 — Ship systems & Power Cells (18 s)

**Hook:** An upgraded Genesis Pulse fills the field with rings of gems.

**Shots:** 0–2.25 s: upgraded Genesis Pulse hook. 2.25–3.75 s: basic Genesis
Pulse before upgrades. 3.75–9.75 s: buying a Power Cell and spending Power Cells
on Genesis Pulse nodes in the `GuiSkin` Ship Systems window. 9.75–18 s: upgraded
Genesis Pulse and Graviton Cascade pulling the haul home.

## 07 — Signals & Shipyard in the Ship Terminal (15 s)

**Hook:** Deep Space Signal choice cards in the new `GuiSkin` Ship Terminal.

**Shots:** 0–3.6 s: scanning and choosing a Deep Space Signal bonus. 3.6–8.4 s:
switching to the Shipyard tab and equipping `Thunder God` and `Rocket Rack` in the
module slots. 8.4–15 s: closing the Ship Terminal to unleash the upgraded fleet and
weapon barrage on the planet.

## 08 — Core Drill, Kamikaze Bombers & Singularity extraction (16 s)

**Hook:** Core Drill beam and Kamikaze Bombers striking the molten planet.

**Shots:** 0–3.6 s: Core Drill and Kamikaze Wing bombardment. 3.6–9.8 s: core
fracture contraction, rainbow gem eruption, and collecting the Core Shard.
9.8–11.8 s: holding Extract Core in the HUD. 11.8–12.8 s: the black hole
Singularity swallowing the planet. 12.8–16 s: late-game Core Drill backdrop for
the end card.

## "The Game Dev's Problem" series (09–13)

Five problem/solution narrative shorts with on-screen white captions, Synthwave
music, and game SFX. No dimmed end card: each short finishes directly on peak
gameplay action.

## 09 — Problem: Gem flood vs. Prospector & Graviton Cascade (18 s)

**Hook:** Full late-game weapon barrage flooding space with gems.

**Shots:** 0–3 s: full weapon barrage (`"In my incremental space game / you crack planets for gems"`).
3–6.5 s: Arc Harpoon & Railgun shockwave spawning dense circular clusters faster than basic ships can collect (`"Problem: weapons spawn gems / faster than ships can collect"`).
6.5–12 s: Prospectors flying into clusters and pulling them in (`"So we added Prospector ships / that pull in whole clusters"`).
12–18 s: upgraded Genesis Pulse and Graviton Cascade chaining the ring home (`"Plus Graviton Cascade / to chain the entire ring home"`).

## 10 — Problem: Fleet traffic jams vs. Five specialized classes (18 s)

**Hook:** Early Drifter harvesters collecting around the inner orbit.

**Shots:** 0–3 s: basic Drifters (`"In my space mining game / you start with basic Drifter ships"`).
3–6 s: crowded Drifter fleet roaming the inner field (`"Problem: Drifters all roam / the same inner field"`).
6–9 s: Rimrunners sweeping the outer orbit (`"So we built 5 ship classes: / Rimrunners sweep the edges"`).
9–12 s: Seekers hunting high-tier gems (`"Seekers hunt high-tier gems"`).
12–15 s: Prospectors scooping clusters (`"Prospectors scoop gem clusters"`).
15–18 s: Trove Hunters warping home (`"And Trove Hunters / warp straight home"`).

## 11 — Problem: Sealed planet crust vs. Magma chain reactions (17.5 s)

**Hook:** Shattering the planet's dark outer armor shell.

**Shots:** 0–3.5 s: crust fracture (`"In my indie space game / planets start sealed in armor"`).
3.5–8 s: cannon hits leaving glowing molten craters on the surface (`"Problem: basic cannon shots / only leave surface craters"`).
8–12.5 s: a heavy hit setting off Magma Detonation and Chain Reaction across nearby craters (`"So craters now prime the rock / for magma chain reactions"`).
12.5–17.5 s: Arc Harpoon, Railgun, and full weapon barrage detonating across the surface (`"And heavy ordnance detonates / the entire surface"`).

## 12 — Problem: What happens when the planet breaks? (15.2 s)

**Hook:** Core Drill beam and Kamikaze Wing bombers striking the cracked planet.

**Shots:** 0–3.6 s: Core Drill and Kamikaze bombardment (`"In my incremental game / the goal is to break the planet"`).
3.6–7.6 s: core fracture freezes the field and pulls loose gems inward (`"Problem: what happens when / the planet actually breaks?"`).
7.6–12 s: rainbow gem eruption and clicking the ejected Core Shard (`"Each fracture erupts / and drops a Core Shard"`).
12–15.2 s: holding Extract Core in the HUD and collapsing the planet into a black hole Singularity (`"Then collapse it into a / black hole to Prestige"`).

## 13 — Problem: Unarmed collector ships vs. Shipyard modules (16.5 s)

**Hook:** Unarmed Drifters roaming the gem field while only the mothership fires.

**Shots:** 0–3.3 s: unarmed Drifters (`"In my space game / your fleet starts out unarmed"`).
3.3–6.5 s: basic collector ships passively gathering gems (`"Problem: collector ships / did zero damage to the planet"`).
6.5–11.3 s: opening the Shipyard tab in the Ship Terminal and equipping `Thunder God` and `Rocket Rack` (`"So we built a Shipyard / to fit weapons on every class"`).
11.3–16.5 s: closing the terminal as the armed fleet unleashes lightning and rockets alongside the mothership (`"Now the whole fleet / fights alongside the mothership"`).
